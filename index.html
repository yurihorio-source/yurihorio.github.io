A photographer's eye for marketing

I studied at Karunia Global School in Jambi and SMA Uphcollege, and I'm now completing my Bachelor's in Business Management at Prasetiya Mulya, expected in 2027.

Photography has been part of my life since school, when I joined the documentation team. In 2022 I interned at Alexander Photostudio, shooting graduation and passport photos and learning to edit. Today I design social media content and presentation decks for my university organisation.

All of it taught me one lesson: a message only works when it speaks to its audience. My goal is to grow into a brand manager who combines consumer insight with creative craft, starting with a graduate marketing programme at a consumer goods company.
